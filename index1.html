Why CometJet

More than a virtual airline.

A community-driven airline built around useful tools, structured operations and the freedom to enjoy simulation without turning it into a second job.

01

Community-First Focus

For us, our community is the ultimate priority. Experience an active, tight-knit environment with frequent VC sessions where nobody ever feels like they're flying alone.

  • Active Voice Channels
  • Deep Human Connection
  • No Lone Flying Guarantee
02

Advanced Pilot Portal

We maximize Newsky capabilities to bring you a fully integrated, professional dispatch and tracking suite designed for maximum immersion.

  • SimBrief & Newsky Integration
  • Interactive Checklists & NOTAMs
  • Stats & Leaderboards
  • Download Center & Pilot Academy
03

3 Years of Proven Growth

Backed by 3 years of continuous operation, CometJet offers structured progression, stability and constant motivation to develop your virtual aviation career.

  • 3 Years of Operations
  • Continuous Career Motivation
  • Professional Fleet & Schedules
04

Your Flight. One EFB.

CometEFB is our dedicated in-flight companion built specifically for CometJet pilots. From live Newsky flight status and SimBrief briefings to airport charts, weather, VATSIM traffic and airline statistics — the essentials stay together in one tablet-friendly interface.

  • Live Newsky Flight Status & Summaries
  • Full SimBrief OFP & Flight Data
  • Integrated Airport Charts
  • METAR, TAF & VATSIM Integration
  • Built for Desktop & Tablet
